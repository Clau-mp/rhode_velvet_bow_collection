THE VELVET BOW POCKET BLUSH

Our three pocket blush of the collection

A little blush for a little flush. Our on-the-go cream blush wakes up cheeks and lips with a satiny, diffused flush that melts on smoothly and stays all day. It’s a lightweight formula that hydrates skin for baby-soft cheeks and leaves a glowy finish without a greasy feel. Size: 5.3 g / 0.18 oz.

BENEFITS +

• Long-wearing flush that won’t budge
• Hydrates skin for baby-soft cheeks
• Doesn’t leave a greasy or sticky feel

APPLICATION +

Apply with your finger or a brush and tap onto cheeks. Color is buildable, so tap on a little or a lot—however you like it. For a monochromatic look, apply to cheeks and lips. Avoid the eye area.

RHODE TRICKS +

• For a monochromatic look, apply to cheeks and lips
• For a glossy pout, apply to lips then top it off with a swipe of Peptide Lip Tint
• For a glazed finish, pat the tiniest bit of Peptide Glazing Fluid on top
